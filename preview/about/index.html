About

I am in my final year of a BA in International History at LSE, most interested in foreign policy, fiscal policy and political risk. The degree is not a quantitative one, but I have a quantitative side to it: A-Level Maths and Further Maths, a First in Microeconomics, and a research project on corruption and foreign direct investment that I am currently designing.

Outside the degree I am volunteer boat and shore crew with the RNLI at Littlestone Lifeboat Station in Kent, which my family has been involved with since 1967. I am also inducted at Tower Lifeboat Station in London so that I can keep crewing during term. I have served as Charity Officer and then Secretary of LSESU RAG, on a committee of over forty.

I read the FT daily and have done since I was thirteen, and I go and sit in on Select Committee hearings in Parliament, which is where a lot of what I write starts. Those notes are here.

I am looking for a graduate role starting in 2027, in policy or political risk advisory, or somewhere adjacent in research, consulting or finance. If any of this is useful to you, write to me at samuel.leigh147@gmail.com.
