## The brief

Missang Advisory is a Westminster-based geostrategy and critical minerals advisory. The
question was the UK's sovereign-level framework for minerals partnerships with Africa, and
specifically its near-total absence.

## What we did

Working in a student consulting team, we benchmarked the leading international frameworks
against criteria of policy design and stakeholder engagement. The main comparators were the
US Minerals Security Partnership and the EU Critical Raw Materials Act. From that we built
recommendations for how the client could engage with and shape UK policy.

We delivered the report and presented the findings to the client.
