**Status: scoping and research design.** No quantitative methods have been applied and no
country cases have been selected. What follows describes a study being designed, not results.

## The question

How host-country corruption affects flows of foreign direct investment.

## Where it has got to

The work so far has been about defining what a good answer would look like: which
classifications to use, which corruption indices are defensible, and what time period the data
can actually support. I have been working with three sources: fDi Markets, the World Bank
Enterprise Surveys, and Transparency International's Corruption Perceptions Index.

I have delivered two presentations to the LSESU Economics Society on the research question,
the design considerations, and the proposed methodology.

## What comes next

A literature review, a data audit, and a decision between a global panel design and a single
region. If I narrow it, the candidates are Latin America and Sub-Saharan Africa.

## Why this page says so little

Because the empirical work has not started. If you are reading this after the working paper
exists, the page will say so.
